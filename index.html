About Us

Hi, I’m Emily, the proud owner and professional dog walker behind Reddy for Walkie. With a genuine love for dogs and a passion for their well-being, I’ve created a dog walking service that prioritises safety, trust, and happiness.


Whether it’s a lively group walk or a calm solo stroll, my goal is to make every outing enriching, enjoyable, and perfectly suited to your dog’s individual needs. I take the time to understand each dog’s personality, energy level, and preferences—ensuring every walk is a positive experience from start to finish.


At Reddy for Walkies, it’s more than just walking—it’s about building lasting bonds, providing consistent care, and giving you peace of mind knowing your dog is in safe and experienced hands. Whether you’ve got a playful pup, a dog a little more triggered by the world or a shy senior, they’ll always be treated with patience, love, and respect.

Services

Solo Walks


Our Solo walks are for any dog, a one-to-one walk tailored to your dogs needs. Some dogs prefer their own space and like to go on a walks perfected for them. Ideal for more nervous dogs, dogs triggered by the world a little more or dogs just needing a little more attention.


All our dog walks start with a Solo walk, this give us a chance to get to know your dog on a personal level before we can integrate them into a group. Building a bond and a good level of trust with your dog is our priority. This allows for better recall and interactions when out on our walks.


These walks are also perfect for reactive dogs, we can tailor you walk to suit your dogs needs. Walking at quieter times and places while your dog still has a satisfying walk.


30 mins - £10

60 mins - £18

(+£5 for each extra dog in the household)

Group Walks


Our Group walks are currently not running. As our business is new we will be focusing on our Solo walks till we can start to build the group walks.


Our Group walks are for the more sociable dogs who like to have a companion. Our groups go up to 4 dogs walked at one time mostly off-lead (subject on dogs recall).


Fun, social walks with a small group of friendly dogs. Great for exercise, enrichment, and boosting confidence. Dogs will be chosen for our dog groups on temperament, age, and play-style. All new dogs will be walked on a solo walk basis till the dog walker is comfortable with which group your dog can join.


When introducing a new dog into a group or when starting whole new group, secure dog fields will be used at no extra cost. This is so the Dog walker is able to test recall and trust without the risk of a dog getting lost.


60 mins - £18

Puppy Visits & Pop-ins


Our puppy visits provide short walks with playtime, toilet breaks, and basic training support. Perfect for young pups still building stamina.


Depending on the age of your puppy we can provide walks if they are fully vaccinated. We also provide in home interaction where we can pop in spend some time with your puppy, allowing for toilet breaks, playtime and basic training.


Our puppy visits are adapted to suit you and your puppy


Our pop ins include Quick visits for toilet breaks, fuss, and a stretch in the garden. Ideal for dogs that don’t need a full walk.


15 mins - £5

30 mins - £10
